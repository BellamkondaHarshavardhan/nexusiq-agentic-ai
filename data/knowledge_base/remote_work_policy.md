# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid Schedule
Nexus Technologies follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office **3 days per week**: **Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday**. Mondays and Fridays are flexible remote days. The Hyderabad headquarters is located in HITEC City.

## Core Hours
Everyone must be reachable during **core hours, 11:00 to 16:00 IST**, wherever they are working from.

## Work From Anywhere
Employees may work from another city or country for up to **20 days per calendar year** with written approval from their manager and HR. Working from abroad needs approval at least **15 days in advance** for tax and legal checks.

## Home-Office Support
- One-time ergonomic setup allowance: **Rs. 15,000** (desk, chair or monitor stand).
- Monthly internet reimbursement: **Rs. 2,000** for employees working remotely at least 2 days a week.
- Company laptops are provided to every employee; personal devices may not be used for client data.

## Remote Work Expectations
Keep your calendar updated, join video meetings with your camera on for team stand-ups, and respond to messages within a reasonable time. Managers review remote-work arrangements every quarter.
