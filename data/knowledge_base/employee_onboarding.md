# Employee Onboarding Guide

## Before Day 1
HR sends the offer documents, background-verification form and the Day 1 schedule one week before your joining date.

## Day 1
You receive your laptop, ID card, and accounts for email, chat and the NexusHR portal. A **buddy** from your team is assigned to help you settle in, and your manager holds a welcome meeting.

## First Week
- Complete the mandatory **information-security training** and the code-of-conduct course.
- Set up MFA on all accounts.
- Meet the key people in your team and in adjacent departments.

## 30-60-90 Day Plan
- **First 30 days:** learn the product, tools and processes.
- **By 60 days:** own a small deliverable end to end.
- **By 90 days:** contribute independently and agree goals with your manager.

## Probation
The probation period is **6 months**. A review happens at the end of month 3 and month 6. During probation the notice period is **30 days**.

## Learning and Development
Every employee gets a learning budget of **Rs. 40,000 per year** for courses, certifications and conferences, plus free access to the company learning platform. Cloud and AI certifications are fully reimbursed after successful completion.
