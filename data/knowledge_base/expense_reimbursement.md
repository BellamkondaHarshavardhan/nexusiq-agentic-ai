# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Submitting Claims
Submit expense claims in the NexusExpense app within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 30 days need Finance approval. Receipts are mandatory for any expense above **Rs. 500**.

## Approval Limits
- Up to **Rs. 25,000**: approved by your reporting manager.
- Above **Rs. 25,000**: manager approval plus Finance approval.
Approved claims are reimbursed within **10 working days**.

## Business Travel Limits
- Flights: economy class. Book at least 7 days ahead through the company travel desk.
- Hotels: up to **Rs. 8,000 per night** in metro cities and Rs. 5,000 elsewhere.
- Meals: up to **Rs. 1,500 per day** during domestic business travel.
- Local transport: cabs and metro are reimbursed at actuals with receipts.

## Not Reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades booked without approval are not reimbursable.

## Corporate Cards
Employees who travel frequently can request a corporate card from Finance. Personal use of the card is not allowed and must be repaid within 15 days.
