# HR Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Nexus Technologies.

## Annual Leave
Every employee receives **24 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to **10 unused days** can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 December.

## Sick Leave
Employees get **12 days of paid sick leave** per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Casual Leave and Holidays
There are **6 days of casual leave** per year for urgent personal matters. The company observes **12 public holidays** each year plus **2 optional floating holidays** that employees can choose from the holiday calendar.

## Parental Leave
- Maternity leave: **26 weeks** of paid leave.
- Paternity leave: **10 working days** of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to Apply
Apply through the NexusHR portal at least **3 working days in advance** for any leave longer than 2 days. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. Emergency leave can be reported by phone and regularised in the portal afterwards.

## Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is **60 days**. Managers and above serve a **90-day** notice period. Unused annual leave is encashed at the final settlement.
