# Employee Benefits

## Health Insurance
Every employee is covered from **day one** by a group health insurance policy with a sum insured of **Rs. 5 lakh** for the employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents can be added with a small monthly premium contribution. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals in all major cities.

## Life and Accident Cover
Group term life insurance equal to **3 times annual fixed salary** and personal accident cover of Rs. 10 lakh are provided at no cost.

## Provident Fund
The company contributes **12% of basic salary** to the provident fund, matching the employee contribution.

## Wellness
- Annual health check-up for employees above 30.
- **Employee assistance programme** with free confidential counselling, available 24x7.
- Subsidised cafeteria at the Hyderabad office and a monthly wellness allowance of Rs. 1,000.

## Other Perks
Referral bonus of **Rs. 30,000** for successful referrals who complete 6 months, and an annual team off-site.
