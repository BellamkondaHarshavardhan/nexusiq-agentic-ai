# Compensation Bands and Review Cycle - HR Confidential

This document is restricted to the HR team. Do not share it with employees or managers.

## Salary Bands (annual fixed pay)
- **L1 Associate:** Rs. 6 to 10 lakh
- **L2 Engineer / Analyst:** Rs. 10 to 18 lakh
- **L3 Senior:** Rs. 18 to 30 lakh
- **L4 Lead / Manager:** Rs. 30 to 45 lakh
- **L5 Director:** Rs. 45 lakh and above, individually negotiated

## Variable Pay
Annual performance bonus is **8% to 15%** of fixed pay depending on the rating. Sales roles have a separate commission plan with a 70:30 fixed-to-variable split.

## Review Cycle
Performance reviews run in **April**, and salary revisions take effect from **1 May**. The standard increment pool is **9%**, with up to 20% for top performers. Promotions are decided by the calibration committee in March.

## Equity
Employees at L3 and above are eligible for ESOPs vesting over **4 years with a 1-year cliff**.

## Offer Approvals
Offers above the band maximum need written approval from the Head of HR and the CFO.
