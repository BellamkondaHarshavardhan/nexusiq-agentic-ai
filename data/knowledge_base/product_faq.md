# Nexus Products FAQ

## What products does Nexus Technologies sell?
Nexus sells software in five categories: **CRM** (Nexus CRM Pro and Lite), **Analytics** (Analytics Studio and Insight Dashboards), **AI** (Nexus AI Copilot and Vision API), **Security** (Nexus Shield and Identity) and **Automation** (Flow Automation and Connect Integrations).

## How is pricing structured?
Products are priced per seat per year, in US dollars. Volume discounts of 5% to 10% apply to deals above 50 seats and are approved by the Sales Manager.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every product has a **14-day free trial** with sample data. Trials can be extended once by 14 days with the approval of an Account Manager.

## What support plans are available?
- **Standard:** business hours (8x5), first response within 8 hours.
- **Premium:** 24x7 support, first response within **1 hour** for priority-1 issues.
Both plans include access to the knowledge portal and quarterly product reviews.

## What is the uptime commitment?
The cloud services carry a **99.9% monthly uptime SLA**. Service credits apply if uptime falls below the commitment.

## Where is customer data stored?
Customers can choose data residency in **India (Hyderabad), the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia)**. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

## How do I escalate a customer issue?
Raise a ticket in the support desk and tag the Customer Success Manager. Priority-1 outages are escalated to the on-call engineering manager via the incident hotline.
